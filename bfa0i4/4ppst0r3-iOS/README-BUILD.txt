4ppst0r3 iOS wrapper project

Open 4ppst0r3.xcodeproj in Xcode on a Mac.
Select the 4ppst0r3 target > Signing & Capabilities > choose your Apple ID Team.
Change the bundle identifier if needed, then Build/Run on a device or Product > Archive.
For an IPA: Organizer > Distribute App > Custom (or Development/Ad Hoc) > Export.

The app bundles the website locally in StoreWeb. Its LiveContainer handoff is routed to iOS.
Minimum deployment target: iOS 15.0.
